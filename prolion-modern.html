What we do

Everything your numbers need, in one relationship.

No more juggling a bookkeeper here, a tax person there and a spreadsheet you don't trust. One partner across the whole finance lifecycle — from keeping the books clean to modelling your next funding round.

01

Bookkeeping

Clean, current records kept month to month, so you always know where the money is — and audit season holds no surprises.

02

Tax returns & compliance

CIT, VAT, WHT and PAYE prepared, computed and filed correctly with the NRS — deadlines met, penalties avoided.

03

Management accounts & reporting

Periodic P&L, balance sheet and cash flow that tell you how the business is really performing, month after month.

04

Financial statements

IFRS-aligned year-end accounts and notes, fit to put in front of a bank, an investor or a regulator with confidence.

05

Software implementation

Set-up, migration and training on QuickBooks, Sage, Zoho or Odoo — so your books run on a real system, not a spreadsheet.

06

Financial modelling

Forecasts, budgets, valuations and scenario models that stand up to scrutiny — for decisions, funding rounds and grant bids.

07

Data & business analytics

Dashboards and analysis that turn raw transactions into the few numbers that actually drive your decisions.

08

Audit & tax authority support

Pre-audit workbooks, objection letters and reconciliations built to withstand an NRS review when it comes.

09

Advisory retainers

Your own finance team on call, every month — the full function, without the cost of hiring one.
